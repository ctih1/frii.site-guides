# Referral Codes

## What are referral codes?
Referral codes let you invite new people to **frii.site**.  
When someone clicks your referral link, a cookie is stored in their browser. If they sign up and verify their account, both you and the new user get rewarded. 🎉  

## Rewards
- Both you and the referred user receive **one extra domain**.  

## How to create a referral code
1. Go to [your account settings](https://canary.frii.site/account/manage).  
2. In the **"Referrals"** section, create a new referral code.  

> [!NOTE]
> Once created, you cannot remove or replace the code.  
> The code may only include **letters, numbers, and hyphens (`-`)**.  

3. Press the **"Create"** button.  
4. You’ll get a referral link (e.g., `https://canary.frii.site/login?ref=your-code`) to share with others.  

## How to share your code
- Send your referral link to friends, colleagues, or communities.  
- When someone clicks it, a cookie is stored in their browser for **2 weeks**, even if they leave the page.  
- If they sign up and verify their account within that time, both of you get the reward.  

## Important notes
> [!IMPORTANT]
> Do not create multiple accounts for yourself.  
> If detected, **all of your accounts will be terminated** and any associated domains deleted.
