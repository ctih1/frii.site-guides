# How do I get more domains?

There are several ways you can increase your domain limit.

1. Join giveaways & events
    > We host giveaways and events on our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/ANeVwQ5yWq). 

2. Contact our support team
    > If you have a good cause for needing more domains (such as school projects), you can request more domains by emailing us at contact@frii.site

3. Get people to use frii.site with referrals
> Read more: https://guides.frii.site/faq/referrals.html
