# Emails not being delivered
This most commonly happens when you're registering for an account.

## Possible reasons:
* **Your mail server does not support `STARTTLS` (aka doesn't support TLS)**
* **Email flagged as spam**
    * Check your spam folder
        * If the email was marked as spam, it is possible that your provider outright hid the email
    * **Can usually be solved** by adding `send@frii.site` to a whitelist


## Fixes not working?
We can manually verify your account. Email us from the email you used during sign up, and we will verify your account.
